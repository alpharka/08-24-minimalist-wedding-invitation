# Minimalist Wedding Invitation - Design Philosophy

## Chosen Approach: "Timeless Elegance"

This design embodies the principle of "Less, but better" through refined simplicity and premium restraint.

### Design Movement
**Modern Minimalism with Classical Proportions** — inspired by contemporary luxury brands (Céline, Brunello Cucinelli) that pair extreme simplicity with timeless craftsmanship. The aesthetic draws from Swiss design's grid discipline and Japanese *ma* (negative space as content).

### Core Principles
1. **Negative Space as Content** — Whitespace is not empty; it's breathing room that elevates every element. Each section is surrounded by generous margins that create rhythm and focus.
2. **Typographic Hierarchy Over Decoration** — All visual distinction comes from font choice, weight, and scale. Playfair Display (elegant serif) for headings conveys formality; Inter (clean sans-serif) for body text ensures readability.
3. **Restrained Luxury** — The gold accent (#D4AF37) appears sparingly—only in dividers, subtle borders, and key CTAs—never as a background or dominant color.
4. **Vertical Flow** — Content moves downward in a single, uninterrupted column (max 900px), creating a meditative, intentional reading experience.

### Color Philosophy
- **Primary (#FFFFFF)**: Pure white as the dominant background. It's not neutral—it's a statement of clarity and premium positioning.
- **Secondary (#F8F8F8)**: Barely-off-white for subtle card backgrounds and section separators. The minimal contrast creates visual rest without jarring shifts.
- **Text (#1F1F1F)**: Deep charcoal, not pure black. Reduces eye strain and feels warmer than #000000.
- **Accent (#D4AF37)**: Warm gold used as a signature detail—thin dividers, button borders, and emphasis lines. It signals elegance without overwhelming.
- **Border (#EAEAEA)**: Barely-visible gray for subtle structure. Borders are felt, not seen.

**Emotional Intent**: The palette whispers rather than shouts. It conveys confidence through restraint and sophistication through simplicity.

### Layout Paradigm
**Centered Vertical Composition** — Every section is centered within a max-width container (900px). Content flows top-to-bottom with:
- Large top/bottom padding (80px+ on desktop, 60px+ on mobile) between sections
- Centered text alignment for headings and CTAs
- Cards and form elements centered with subtle shadows
- No sidebars, no asymmetric layouts—the focus is entirely on the couple and their celebration

### Signature Elements
1. **Thin Gold Dividers** — Horizontal lines (1px, #D4AF37) separating sections. They're delicate, not heavy, and appear above/below key sections.
2. **Subtle Leaf Ornaments** — Tiny, minimalist leaf outlines (SVG, 20-30px) centered above section headings. They're barely noticeable but add warmth.
3. **Generous Whitespace Blocks** — Large empty areas between sections (120px+ on desktop) create visual breathing room and emphasize the "less is more" philosophy.

### Interaction Philosophy
- **Soft Transitions** — All interactions (button hover, form focus, section entrance) use 200-300ms fade and subtle slide-up animations.
- **Responsive Feedback** — Buttons scale down slightly (0.97) on click; form inputs gain a gold underline on focus.
- **No Distraction** — Animations are smooth and understated. No bounce, no parallax, no dramatic effects. The focus remains on the content.

### Animation
- **Entrance**: Fade-in + slide-up (200-300ms) for sections as they scroll into view
- **Hover**: Subtle opacity change (100ms) on buttons and interactive elements
- **Focus**: Thin gold underline appears on form inputs (150ms ease-out)
- **Button Press**: Scale to 0.97 with 160ms ease-out, then back to 1.0 on release
- **No Bounce, No Parallax** — All animations are linear or ease-out. Simplicity is the goal.

### Typography System
- **Headings**: Playfair Display (serif, elegant, 48px-72px on desktop, 32px-48px on mobile)
  - Font weights: 400 (regular), 700 (bold) for emphasis
  - Line-height: 1.2 for tight, elegant spacing
  - Letter-spacing: -0.02em for refined appearance
  
- **Body Text**: Inter (sans-serif, readable, 16px-18px on desktop, 14px-16px on mobile)
  - Font weights: 400 (regular), 500 (medium for emphasis), 600 (semi-bold for labels)
  - Line-height: 1.6 for comfortable reading
  - Letter-spacing: 0 (default)

- **Hierarchy Rules**:
  - H1 (Couple Names): Playfair Display, 72px, 700, letter-spacing -0.02em
  - H2 (Section Titles): Playfair Display, 48px, 400, letter-spacing -0.01em
  - H3 (Subsections): Playfair Display, 32px, 400
  - Body: Inter, 16px, 400, line-height 1.6
  - Labels/Captions: Inter, 14px, 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em

### Brand Essence
**One-line Positioning**: A timeless celebration of love, designed for couples who believe elegance lies in simplicity.

**Personality Adjectives**: Refined, Serene, Intentional

### Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Warm, respectful, and understated. No clichés like "Welcome to our celebration" or "Join us for the big day."
- **Headlines**: Poetic but grounded. Example: "Two hearts, one story" instead of "Our Wedding"
- **CTAs**: Gentle and inviting. Example: "Confirm your presence" instead of "RSVP Now"
- **Microcopy**: Thoughtful and personal. Example: "We'd be honored to celebrate with you" instead of "Please respond"

### Wordmark & Logo
**Logo Concept**: A minimalist ampersand (&) rendered in Playfair Display, 64px, #D4AF37, on a transparent background. It's elegant, timeless, and universally understood as the symbol of togetherness.

### Signature Brand Color
**Gold (#D4AF37)** — Warm, luxurious, and restrained. It appears in thin dividers, button borders, and accent details. Never as a background or dominant color.

## Design Decisions (from Style Review)
- Maintain strict vertical centering for all sections
- Use generous whitespace (120px+ between sections on desktop)
- Playfair Display for all headings; Inter for all body text
- Gold accent appears only in dividers, borders, and CTAs
- All animations are 200-400ms, fade/slide-up only
- No decorative elements beyond thin dividers and tiny leaf ornaments
